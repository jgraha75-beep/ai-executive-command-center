# Monthly Strategy Review

**Month:**

1. Which objectives improved? What is the evidence?
2. Which areas consumed time but produced little value?
3. Has the bottleneck changed?
4. What should receive more capacity next month? What should receive less?
5. Are goals, constraints, or assumptions now outdated?
6. Which domains should remain active, become dormant, merge, or be audited?
7. Which agent workflows or automations genuinely saved time or improved quality?
8. What financial, health, academic, or deadline risks need early action?
9. Update the 30/90-day objective view and success metrics.

End with a maximum of three strategic changes—not a total system redesign.
