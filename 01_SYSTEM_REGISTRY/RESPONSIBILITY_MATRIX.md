## Authority tiers

| Tier | Existing authoritative locations | Purpose | Change authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Core Authority | Executive mission, project instructions, operating principles, decision framework, registry and responsibility rules | Objectives, governing policy, core architecture, permanent routing principles | Category C workflow, verification, and Jahmeir approval. |
| 2 — Operational State | Current State, Master Action Queue, Decision Ledger, Risk Register, Waiting Queue, and domain returns | Current tasks, priorities, handoffs, live decisions, constraints, and blockers | Executive and domain operations may update through normal verified work. |
| 3 — Advisory Data | Knowledge Layer, research cache, `AI_OPERATIONS_CALIBRATION.md`, raw/archive history | Evidence that may inform but never override authority; Knowledge Layer entries are reusable only within recorded scope and freshness limits | May be added or ignored; cannot silently change Tier 1 policy. |

When sources disagree, Tier 1 governs policy and the newest verified Tier 2 record governs current operational facts. Tier 3 is not normal working context.
